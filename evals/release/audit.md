## How it behaves

### Reading and deciding

Each row names the evidence, severity, existing draft, proposed repair, affected files and required verification.
**Fix** accepts that named proposal and any listed pre-hold draft; it authorizes the work and its checks.
**Defer** records Indy's reason and remaining blockage; a deferred item cannot be reported as repaired.
**Leave** records Indy's decision to retain the observed behavior and its stated consequence.
No pending row has repair approval.

A recommendation is Aiwa's judgment. Indy's approval is the decision.
For overlapping rows, approve the combined file changes once and verify every affected finding.
The linked [evaluation plan](evaluation-plan.md) supplies the semantic and autonomous-task trial design; proposed trials have no passing results yet.

High issues threaten owner bytes, authorization, secrecy, gate integrity or publication correctness.
Medium issues weaken supported behavior, audit accuracy, coverage or resource control.
Low issues are bounded maintainability problems.
Scope rows record existing owner decisions and require no new provider or native-build authorization.
